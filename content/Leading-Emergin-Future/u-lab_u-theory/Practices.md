Module 1: Co-initiation 

#### caminhada-da-empatia
This practice has been adapted from the work of MIT Professor Emeritus Ed Schein whose work shaped the field of organizational culture, learning and leadership. The purpose of the Empathy walk is to:

1. 1. To develop empathy for someone very different from yourself
    2. To develop your skill in establishing a relationship across a significant boundary

In this exercise, you will connect with another person whose experiences, perspectives and views are different from your own and engage in a conversation with the intention to understand reality from their perspective.

Take a "dialogue walk" with another person to explore what is emerging for each of you now doesn't matter if your dialogue partner is in u-lab or not.

A dialogue walk is normally done in pairs. One person shares a story while the other listens without interruption. Given quality space and time to think, people discover surprising insights and wisdom within themselves. Each person should plan to speak for about 10-15 minutes. Total, the conversation should last 20-30 minutes. The following are suggestions for what you might do together during the dialogue walk:

1. 1. 1. Ask your partner if they might be willing to share a story from their life when something happened that clarified, deepened or changed their sense of who they really are.
        2. Then ask your partner, what is important in your life and work right now? What shifts or changes are showing up?
        3. Practice your level 3 and level 4 listening - open mind and open heart - just as you did during the empathy walk.
        4. When it's your turn to speak, share the seeds that are emerging for you now. What has your best energy and attention? What shifts or changes are showing up? Where do you feel the seed of the future? 

After the dialogue walk, take some time on your own to reflect:

- - - What struck me most? What surprised me?
        - What touched me?
        - Is there any action that this dialogue invites me to step into?

Enjoy the conversations.

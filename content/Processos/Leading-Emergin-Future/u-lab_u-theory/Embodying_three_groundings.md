Co-evolving. Here we are in the final phase of our U-Lab cycle.

What is it that is supporting us going forward?

When we talk about co-evolving, we
are not only talking about the evolution of a prototype.
What we are really talking about is the sustained activation
of a regenerative social field.

In Theory U, we call that a level 4 field
or a generative social field.

What that means is that within the system
there is a certain quality of presence and connectedness
in at least three directions of grounding.

Horizontal grounding, extending our connection
not only interpersonally, but also
cross organizationally towards including the entire ecosystem
of relationships.

Vertical grounding.
Clarifying our sense of purpose upward,
and also our sense of place downward.
The place we are standing on and that is holding us.

Temporal grounding.
Strengthening the presence of the past,
and the presence of the emerging future in the current moment
with a bias towards the emerging future, because that's
the aspects that's often is being missed.
So the three keys of co-evolving are, one,
to embody the three groundings in everything
we do as a person.

How do these three groundings show up personally most often?
Through transforming our qualities of listening.
How do we deepen our listening to others, to ourselves,
and to what is wanting to emerge through us
from our own experience?

Secondly, to embody the three groundings in everything
you do as an organization or as a team.
How do these groundings show up on a face to face level?
Through transforming our qualities of conversation.
How we shift and deepen the level
of conversation from downloading and debate
to dialogue and collective creativity.

And three, to embody the three groundings in everything
we do on a whole systems level.
How do these groundings show up on a whole system's level?
Through transforming our qualities and mechanisms
of governance. That is, through interiorizing the larger systems issues
that we use to outsource to abstract exterior
forces of markets, of hierarchies, or algorithms
towards including them in our awareness
that we use for individual and for collective decision making.

So the key to co-evolving lies in embodying
the three groundings in all our actions
across all system levels.
As individuals, by deepening our listening.
As teams, by deepening our conversations.
And as larger ecosystems, by exteriorizing our governance.
So take a moment and reflect on what
has been emerging from your prototype
and your U-Lab process, and how it can be sustained
and continue to grow through the support of these groundings
and the practices that come with them.
